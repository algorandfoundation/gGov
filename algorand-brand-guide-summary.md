## Brand Philosophy

"Open-source code. Open-source creative. It's your Algorand."

Algorand's brand identity centers on being **borderless** - breaking down barriers in traditional finance. The visual system expresses this through bold, cutting-edge style matched to a vision of a limitless future.

## Logo

### Mark
- A distinctive **triangular mark** composed of three interlocking elements forming a continuous loop (the dynamic "A").
- Clean lines and angular geometry communicate technical sophistication while remaining visually approachable.
- The three-sided structure represents the **blockchain trilemma**: security, scalability, and decentralization.

### Wordmark
- All-lowercase letterforms ("algorand") in a geometric sans-serif typeface.
- Revised logotype uses **bold geometry** — simple, modern, with letterform modifications that create cohesion with the dynamic "A" mark through shared weight and angles.

### Usage Rules
- The Algorand logo and standalone "A" are **trademarks of Algorand Technologies**.
- Available for community and network user use for **non-profit purposes** (including merchandise).
- **Do not** misrepresent the logo in any official Algorand capacity.
- **Do not** combine the logo or name with other phrases or words (e.g., "AlgorandInvesting").
- **Do not** connect Algorand branding to other products/services unless in the context of an application built on the Algorand blockchain.

## Color Palette

| Color            | Hex       | Role     | Notes                                                       |
|------------------|-----------|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Mine Shaft       | `#242424` | Primary  | Dark/black — represents security and protocol strength      |
| White            | `#FFFFFF` | Primary  | Light backgrounds and reversed logo usage                   |
| Caribbean Green  | `#00BEA5` | Accent   | Teal/green — energetic, modern, signature Algorand color    |
| Black            | `#000000` | Neutral  | Pure black for high-contrast contexts                       |

### Additional Color Notes
- **Black CMYK**: (60, 40, 40, 100)
- **Black Pantone**: PMS Black 6 C
- Black was chosen to represent the **security** of the protocol. It provides power and elegance, creating a strong visual presence.
- The accent teal/green shades create a sense of **innovation and dependability**.

## Typography

- **Custom Algorand typeface** used for branding, marketing materials, and the wordmark.
- Based on a **geometric sans-serif** foundation.
- Precise letterforms complement the mathematical nature of the logo symbol.
- The all-lowercase wordmark creates a modern, approachable tone despite technical complexity.

## Visual System

### Grid System
A design grid built from the **angles of the Algorand logomark** serves as the foundation for all visual elements. This grid:
- Unifies the brand across touchpoints
- Creates a sense of movement and dynamic object relationships
- Informs layout, illustration, and pattern design

### Backdrops & Windows
- **Soft, horizonless backdrops** juxtaposed with **vibrant windows** into other visual planes.
- The contrast expresses the "borderless" brand narrative.

### Dark Mode
A dedicated **dark mode** visual treatment designed to make developers feel at home within the brand. Used across developer-facing materials and documentation.
